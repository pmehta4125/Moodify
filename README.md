🎶 Moodify
Moodify is an Android application that connects emotions with music.  
It’s built to make your day better by playing songs that match how you feel — whether you’re happy, sad, relaxed, or just want to vibe.  
The app creates a personalized space where users can freely express their mood through music and thoughts.
It’s more than just a music player — it’s a reflection of how you feel, designed to lift your spirit and make every moment sound better.  
Moodify aims to bring positivity and balance to your daily life through the power of emotions and melodies.
